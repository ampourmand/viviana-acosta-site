In ecologically devastated Earth, a first-time Space Academy teen cadet from the city's lowest strata battles impostorism to pass her spacecraft permit, but when she tries to save her younger brother who joins a rebel group led by their older step-sister, her efforts threaten expulsion, forcing her to choose - her family or her future.
