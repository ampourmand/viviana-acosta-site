Currently querying, this 600-word picture book portrays Hubble as a prideful telescope, destined to achieve scientific marvels for the People. When Shuttle delivers her in space, Hubble activates her sensors and after finally opening her eyes, she realizes – she can't see! This book relates a real-life problem on the Hubble Space Telescope primary mirror that needed a relatable complex solution: corrective lenses!
